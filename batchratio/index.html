Scale a recipe

Enter the original servings and ingredient quantities, then choose desired servings or a multiplier. Calculations use the original quantities without accumulated rounding. Unit labels stay as entered: PortionLoom does not convert units. Ingredients such as “to taste” remain unchanged.

Use a decimal point in English and a decimal comma in Spanish or Catalan. Fractions such as 3/4 and mixed fractions such as 1 1/2 are supported. Desired servings must be greater than zero.

Save and back up

The free app supports three saved recipes, unsaved calculations and backups. In Settings, export a backup and choose Save to Files. Keep a separate copy before changing or deleting data. To restore, choose the original backup file, review the preview and confirm replacement. An invalid or unsupported backup is rejected. The recovery controls can restore the pre-import snapshot after a confirmed import.

A recipe’s saved copy changes only when you save it to the library. A batch keeps its own quantities and checklist. Exported backups and files remain wherever you saved or shared them; deleting app data does not delete those copies.

Lifetime unlock and restore

PortionLoom Pro is a one-time unlock for unlimited saved recipes and plans and formatted exports. The purchase screen shows the current Apple price. Restore Purchases checks ownership through Apple and RevenueCat; use the Apple account that owns the purchase. An internet connection is needed to purchase or restore. Existing local data and backups remain available if ownership cannot be confirmed.

For TestFlight testing, use Apple’s test purchase flow. Please report purchase, cancellation and restore results through the support address. Do not send your Apple password.

Contact mosartgames@gmail.com with the app name, iOS version and a description of the problem. Please do not send passwords, purchase credentials or private recipe backups.
