Pre-release TestFlight information. PortionLoom is being tested; this page does not announce an App Store release. Final privacy disclosures and release checks are still in progress.

Recipe data

Recipes, ingredient quantities, saved recipes, batch plans, checklists and language/display preferences are stored locally on your device. PortionLoom has no app account or recipe-sync backend. Recipe content is not sent to Apple or RevenueCat by the app’s purchase integration. You choose whether to export a backup or share content through iOS.

Purchases and technical information

Apple handles payment. RevenueCat validates purchases and supplies entitlement and product information. This processing includes purchase history, an anonymous app installation identifier and technical information such as app/SDK/OS version, device type and language. The installed SDK also sends an Apple vendor identifier in its requests. PortionLoom supplies no name, email address, phone number or custom account identifier to RevenueCat.

There are no active external analytics, advertising or attribution integrations in PortionLoom’s RevenueCat project. RevenueCat’s own purchase dashboard processes commerce information. The app does not use this information for advertising tracking. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy for its processing practices.

The privacy assessment is still in progress, including the retention and store-disclosure classification of technical identifiers. This page does not claim that no data is collected or that final App Store privacy answers have been validated.

Backups, sharing and deletion

iOS controls device backups. Files you export may be stored locally, in iCloud Drive or with another provider you select. Sharing uses the destination you choose. Review that provider’s handling of your files.

Settings provides a confirmed delete-all action for local app data, including recovery snapshots. Removing the app can also remove its local data. Neither action deletes exported files, iOS backups, Apple transaction records or purchase-service records. Keep an independent backup if you need your recipes. Contact us for questions about purchase-service data; do not include sensitive content or credentials.

This support website

The site is static and has no app account or app backend. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process service request information under GitHub’s privacy statement. If you email support, your message is used to respond to your request.

Contact mosartgames@gmail.com with the app name, iOS version and a description of the problem. Please do not send passwords, purchase credentials or private recipe backups.
