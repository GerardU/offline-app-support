Make your first chart

Choose New pattern, import a PNG/JPEG through Photos or Files, or start blank. Set rows, columns, color count and background handling before generating. Transparent cells can remain empty; a white bead is a separate occupied cell.

Edit and follow

Choose Paint, Erase, Sample or Select. Switch to Pan before moving around a zoomed canvas. Each finished brush stroke is one undo step. Row/column fields provide another way to select and edit a cell. Counts and Boards show occupied cells only. Editing a completed board asks to reset its progress.

Back up or move devices

Settings offers a backup with or without source images. Save it in Files. Import backup validates the file, asks before replacing the library and keeps a recovery copy. BeadGrid accepts PNG/JPEG sources up to 8 MB and 20 million pixels; backups are up to 64 MB with up to 40 MB of sources. Do not edit the integrity-protected backup manually.

Purchase help

Lifetime Pro is optional and has no recurring charge. Use Restore purchases with the purchasing Apple account and an internet connection. If the store price is unavailable, try again when connected. A backup cannot grant Pro. Contact support with the app version, iOS version and a description of the issue; omit credentials and private artwork.
