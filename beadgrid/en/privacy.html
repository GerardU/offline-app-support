Your artwork stays on your device

BeadGrid stores patterns, palette labels, board progress, preferences and copies of images you choose in its local app storage. It does not upload your artwork or backups to us. There is no account, advertising, runtime AI, remote catalog or general usage analytics in the app.

Lifetime purchases

Apple handles payment. RevenueCat receives purchase history and transaction/receipt information to validate purchases and provide Lifetime Pro. Its service also processes an anonymous app user identifier and technical purchase context, such as app/SDK version, locale and currency. We do not send a name, email, artwork, advertising identifier or custom customer attributes to RevenueCat. Purchase information supports app functionality and RevenueCat's purchase reporting. We do not use it for advertising tracking. Initial purchase and restore require internet access; recognized ownership is cached by the purchase SDK.

Photos, Files and sharing

Only images you select are imported. The source copy is separate from edited cells. Files, printing and the share sheet are provided by iOS. A backup can include source images if you choose that option. A file you share, save to a cloud provider or send to a printer leaves the app at your direction and is handled by that service. Device or iCloud backups may include local app data according to your Apple settings.

Deletion and recovery

Removing a pattern moves it to Recently Deleted. Delete local data clears the active library and preferences but keeps an on-device recovery copy. Restoring a backup also creates a recovery copy. These copies and retained source files remain in the app's storage until the app is uninstalled; copies you exported elsewhere must be deleted there. Removing local data does not cancel or refund an Apple purchase.

Support and privacy requests

If you email support, the message and address you provide are used to answer your request. Do not send private photos or payment credentials. Contact mosartgames@gmail.com for help or privacy questions. Apple's and RevenueCat's privacy policies describe their handling of commerce data.
