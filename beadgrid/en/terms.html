Craft chart tool

BeadGrid converts your own simple artwork to rectangular fuse-bead charts and lets you edit cells, swatches, counts and board progress. Use artwork you own or are authorized to use. You retain your rights to your content. The app does not supply licensed character packs or manufacturer color catalogs.

Free and Lifetime Pro

Free includes up to three saved patterns of at most 29 × 29 cells, an editable generic palette of up to 32 colors, full counts, board progress and chart export. Lifetime Pro is one optional, non-consumable purchase for more saved patterns, grids up to 116 × 116, board splits and printable cell symbols. It is not a subscription. The local library has a 500-pattern safety limit, including Recently Deleted. Apple supplies the localized price before purchase. Purchase and restore need connectivity; restore uses the Apple account that bought the product. Refunds and payment questions are handled through Apple, subject to applicable consumer rights.

Approximate colors and reading aids

Screen colors are approximate. Edit labels and codes to match your own stock; no manufacturer match is guaranteed. A 29 × 29 board is a configurable example. PDF charts are coordinate/count reading aids, not physical actual-size templates. Check your board dimensions, material quantities and manufacturer's crafting instructions before making your piece.

Keep your work safe

Your library is local. Make backups before uninstalling or changing devices and inspect exported files before relying on them. Recently Deleted and recovery copies help with accidental changes but do not replace your own backup. The app has no server copy of your artwork. For support, contact mosartgames@gmail.com.
