Keep your own charts offline

ChordDeck keeps your own chord charts on your iPhone. There is no song search catalog. Type or import UTF-8 .txt, .cho, .chopro, .chordpro or .pro text, review the preview, and save it.

Key and capo

Sounding pitch equals played shapes plus capo. Changing the current capo updates shapes while retaining sounding pitch unless you also change the target shift. Original source remains available.

Backup and recovery

Export a JSON backup from Settings. Restore validates the complete backup before replacing songs, sets and preferences and retains a local recovery copy. Recover previous data from Settings. Keep an additional copy in Files.

Document limits

Ordinary imported charts: 256 KiB, 4,000 lines, 8,000 characters per line. ChordDeck displayed exports include two additional metadata lines and allow up to 512 KiB and 16,000 characters per line so a full-size transposed chart can be imported again. Backups: 20 MiB.

Lifetime purchase

Twelve saved songs are free. Lifetime Pro is a one-time purchase for additional songs and reusable setlists. First purchase and restore require internet. Previously recognized ownership can work offline. Existing charts and exports remain accessible when ownership is unavailable.

Contact

Include the app version, iPhone/iOS version and what happened. Do not send private charts unless you want to share them.
