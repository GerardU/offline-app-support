ChordDeck privacy

Pre-release app. Purchase testing and final App Store privacy declarations are pending.

Your songs, original import text, setlists, preferences and recovery copy are stored locally. ChordDeck has no account, remote song library, advertising or custom analytics. It does not upload your chord sheets to our support service or RevenueCat.

Apple and RevenueCat process purchase and restore requests. These can include an anonymous app user identifier, the iOS vendor device identifier, transaction and purchase history information, tracking-consent status, and app/device software details needed for commerce. Purchase history is used for receipt validation, entitlement access and purchase reporting in RevenueCat. We do not identify an anonymous purchase customer with an email address or use purchase history for advertising across apps. The app does not add an advertising or analytics integration.

Exports go to destinations you choose in the native share/Files flow. Those destinations have their own privacy practices. Ordinary record deletion and backup restore retain a local recovery copy. The confirmed Delete local data action removes songs, sets, preferences and the recovery copy permanently. Purchase records are managed by Apple/RevenueCat.

Support email includes what you choose to send and your email address; avoid personal information or charts you do not want to share.
