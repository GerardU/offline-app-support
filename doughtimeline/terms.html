Terms

Your chosen timings

DoughTimeline executes user-defined sequential durations. It does not provide recipes, fermentation or readiness predictions, starter-health advice or food-safety guarantees. The timing example is fictional and editable. You remain responsible for choosing and checking the process.

Purchase and availability

Free and lifetime Pro features are described in the app and store listing. Pro is one non-consumable purchase, not a subscription. The Apple store supplies the localized price and handles purchase/refund requests under its terms. First purchase and restore require connectivity. Access depends on recognized ownership; saved records and export remain accessible.

Local data and reminders

Keep your own exported backups. Deleting local data is confirmed and cannot be undone. We cannot recover local records that you have not backed up. Reminder delivery depends on iOS permission, Focus and device settings. Pausing app scheduling cannot pause dough.

License and support

Apple’s standard app license applies to the App Store download. These product-use notes do not replace that license or your statutory rights. Contact the public email below for app support.

Compra y disponibilidad

Las funciones gratuitas y Pro de por vida se describen en la app y la tienda. Pro es una compra no consumible, sin suscripción. La tienda de Apple muestra el precio local y tramita compras y reembolsos según sus condiciones. La primera compra y la restauración necesitan conexión.
