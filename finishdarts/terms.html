Using FinishDarts

FinishDarts is a local scoring and practice aid. Enter the darts actually thrown and check the result against your game’s rules or referee. Checkout routes are legal suggestions, not guaranteed optimal routes. Practice statistics describe completed drill visits and successes; they are not official tournament averages.

Free features and lifetime Pro

Free includes complete one/two-player 301/501 double-out scoring, undo, legal checkout suggestions and the fixed finish drill. Pro is one non-consumable lifetime purchase for the saved finish queue, weakness ordering and full local practice history. There is no subscription. The App Store presents the price and purchase conditions. Purchase and restore need connectivity. Unknown or revoked ownership does not delete local records or remove export access.
