Privacy

HourFolio is in development. This page describes the current app; it does not claim App Store availability or completed release testing.

Work entries, clients, projects, notes, rate snapshots, revisions and settings stay in the app’s local database. HourFolio does not send this work content to a product server or RevenueCat. There is no account, remote synchronization, advertising SDK or work-usage analytics SDK.

Apple and RevenueCat process purchase history to validate purchases, restore access, prevent fraud and provide purchase dashboard analytics. RevenueCat uses an anonymous app user identifier; this app does not provide a name, email, advertising identifier or work content to it. Store/locale/currency and commerce request information may be processed by these services.

Local records remain until you delete them or uninstall the app. A delete/import operation first saves an app-owned recovery backup. Exported/shared backups and reports are outside the app once you save or send them; delete those copies separately. Apple device backup may contain app data according to your device settings. Purchase records follow Apple and RevenueCat retention policies; deleting local work does not revoke a purchase.

These static pages are hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process standard web-request information under its privacy policy. This site has no login, contact form or analytics script.

For help or privacy questions, email mosartgames@gmail.com. Include HourFolio and the issue. Do not send passwords, payment credentials or confidential client records. An email is processed by the recipient’s email service for support.

Privacidad

HourFolio está en desarrollo. Esta página describe la app actual; no afirma que esté disponible en el App Store ni que las pruebas de publicación estén terminadas.

Registros, clientes, proyectos, notas, tarifas, revisiones y ajustes permanecen en la base local. No enviamos ese contenido a un servidor de producto ni a RevenueCat. No hay cuenta, sincronización, SDK publicitario ni SDK de análisis del trabajo.

Apple y RevenueCat aplican sus políticas a las compras; eliminar trabajo no revoca la compra.

GitHub Pages aloja estas páginas y puede procesar información habitual de las solicitudes web según su política. No hay acceso, formulario ni script de análisis.
