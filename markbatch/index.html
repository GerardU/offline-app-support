Getting started

  1. Open Your brand and import a local PNG/JPEG logo, or enter text.
  2. Choose a photo through Photos or Files. Cloud-only originals may need a download by the system picker.
  3. Set the anchor, width, opacity and padding; save a named preset.
  4. Export a single copy for free. Pro can select up to 30 photos and adjust individual photos before export.
  5. Use Export results to share ready copies or retry failed items. Cancelling a share does not mark copies as saved.

Supported limits

Photos: JPEG, PNG or HEIC; 16 megapixels, 6,000 pixels per edge, 40 MB. Logos: PNG/JPEG; 4 megapixels, 5 MB. Text: 1,200 UTF-8 bytes and up to 16 short lines. Batches: 30 photos. Saved presets: one free, up to 100 with Pro.

Backups and recovery

Settings exports presets and owned logos in a .markbatch file. Photos, export batches and purchases are excluded. Restoring asks before replacing presets and preferences and creates a recovery copy first. Restore or save those copies from Settings. Delete local data requires confirmation and also deletes recovery copies; original photos and lifetime purchases are unaffected.

Purchases

Pro is one lifetime purchase with no subscription. The store supplies the localized price. Purchase and restore need a connection. Use Restore purchases with the Apple account used for purchase. Free single-photo export remains available when the product or ownership is unavailable.
