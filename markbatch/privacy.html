MarkBatch — Privacy

Updated 30 September 2026

Local images and presets

MarkBatch processes selected photos, logos and text on your iPhone. It stores owned files, presets, preferences and export results in its app storage. It does not upload this content to the publisher or RevenueCat. Exported copies omit location metadata by default; originals are unchanged.

Purchase information

Apple processes payments. RevenueCat receives purchase history and transaction/receipt information to validate purchases, enable lifetime access and provide purchase reporting. The SDK uses an anonymous app user identifier; MarkBatch has no login and supplies no name, email, advertising identifier or custom user identity to RevenueCat. Purchase information is used for app functionality and RevenueCat purchase reporting, and is not used for advertising tracking.

Network and system actions

First purchase and restore require network access. The system photo/file picker may retrieve a cloud-only file. Sharing sends only the files you choose through the destination you select. Opening a support page uses your browser and the static host may process normal web request information. MarkBatch adds no analytics, advertising, remote catalog or AI service.

Deletion and backups

Delete local data removes MarkBatch presets, owned logos, temporary copies, preferences and recovery copies after confirmation. It does not delete original photos, backups you saved outside the app or Apple/RevenueCat purchase records. Removing the app removes its local workspace according to iOS storage behavior. Contact support for questions about purchase data; Apple and RevenueCat also describe their data practices below.

Support email

If you email support, the publisher receives your message, email address and any attachments you choose to send to respond to your request. Avoid sending private photos or payment credentials.
