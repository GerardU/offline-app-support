The App Store version uses Apple’s Standard End User License Agreement. These product notes describe MarkBatch’s behavior and do not replace that agreement.

Apple Standard EULA

Use photos, logos and text that you are authorized to use. A visible watermark does not establish legal authorship or guarantee theft prevention. Inspect finished copies before sharing; sRGB output and printed colors may differ.

Purchases

Pro is one lifetime purchase with no subscription. The store supplies the localized price. Purchase and restore need a connection. Use Restore purchases with the Apple account used for purchase. Free single-photo export remains available when the product or ownership is unavailable.

Supported limits

Photos: JPEG, PNG or HEIC; 16 megapixels, 6,000 pixels per edge, 40 MB. Logos: PNG/JPEG; 4 megapixels, 5 MB. Text: 1,200 UTF-8 bytes and up to 16 short lines. Batches: 30 photos. Saved presets: one free, up to 100 with Pro.
