Pre-release / TestFlight. BagMosaic is being tested and is not publicly released. Features and availability may change before release.

Pack a household trip

Create a trip, add travelers and bags, then check items as you pack. Shared items count once for the household; other items belong to a traveler. Quantity totals count individual pieces, while checklist progress counts entries. Departure-morning items remain part of the total until packed.

Use Remaining or Departure morning and traveler/bag filters to focus your list. Trips opens active and archived trips. Manage trip contains editing, duplication, archiving, reset and deletion. Reset asks for confirmation and offers Undo.

Free and Pro

Free includes one active trip, the weekend template, basic packing, archived-trip viewing and backup. BagMosaic Pro is a one-time lifetime unlock for additional active trips, the family holiday, camping and business templates, personal templates and PDF sharing/printing. It is not a subscription. The purchase screen shows Apple’s current localized price.

To restore a previous purchase, open Trips → Settings → BagMosaic Pro → Restore purchases with the Apple Account used to buy it. First purchase and restore require internet. If access cannot be confirmed, your saved trips remain on the device. Contact support if restore fails; never send an Apple password.

Back up or move your data

Open Trips → Settings → Backup and export a JSON backup to Files or a destination you choose. Backups contain readable trip, traveler, bag and item data, so keep them private. To import, select a BagMosaic backup, review its trip/item counts and confirm replacement. Import replaces current data; it does not merge trips. A pre-import safety copy is retained locally and can be exported from Settings for recovery.

There is no app account, live family sharing or app-level cloud synchronization. Export a backup before deleting the app or changing devices. Copies saved to Files or another service are managed separately.

Language, appearance and help

English, Spanish and Catalan, plus appearance preferences, are available in Settings. The departure-morning list works inside the app; it does not send notifications.

Email mosartgames@gmail.com with “BagMosaic”, your app/iOS version, what you expected and what happened. A screenshot with personal details hidden can help. Do not send passwords, purchase credentials or a personal backup unless we agree it is necessary.
