Pre-release / TestFlight notice, updated 29 September 2026. This describes the current app’s data handling. The final App Store privacy questionnaire is still under review; this page does not claim that release review is complete.

Your packing data

BagMosaic stores trip names and dates, travelers, bags, packing items, personal templates and preferences in a local database on your device. The app does not upload this packing content to Mosart Games or RevenueCat. It has no app account, advertising SDK, behavior-analytics SDK or app-level cloud synchronization. The app does not request location, contacts, photo-library or notification access.

Purchases and technical information

Apple processes the optional lifetime purchase. RevenueCat verifies purchases and provides purchase status to unlock Pro. RevenueCat receives purchase history and an automatically generated app-user identifier. Its SDK also sends Apple’s vendor device identifier, app and SDK versions, device model, operating-system version, language/locale and store information with service requests. Network services receive connection information such as an IP address. Commerce requests can occur at launch even when you have not purchased Pro.

This information supports purchase verification, entitlement delivery, fraud prevention and commerce reporting. BagMosaic does not provide traveler names, trip details, your email address or an Apple Account password to RevenueCat. It does not use these requests for targeted advertising or cross-app advertising tracking. We do not receive your payment-card details.

Read Apple’s privacy policy, RevenueCat’s privacy policy and RevenueCat’s app privacy guidance for their roles. Removing local app data does not erase Apple’s transaction records or RevenueCat’s purchase records.

Export, deletion and retention

Export a JSON backup from Trips → Settings → Backup. Sharing or printing a backup/checklist sends it only to the destination you choose through iOS. Those destinations may have their own privacy practices. The app removes temporary export files after the share or print operation finishes; copies you save elsewhere remain under your control.

Import creates a local safety copy before replacing your database. The app retains up to three pre-import safety copies. Trips → Settings → Delete all data clears the current database content and these safety copies after confirmation. Individual trip deletion does not remove a prior safety copy or an exported file. iOS device backups may retain app data according to your device settings. Delete exported copies and device backups separately if needed.

Support and this website

If you email support, we receive your email address and what you choose to include so we can respond. Avoid sensitive content. For privacy questions or a request concerning support/commerce records, contact mosartgames@gmail.com. We will explain what records we can identify and the applicable request process; a request does not itself cancel or refund a purchase.

This static support site contains no analytics scripts or account form. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process service-request information under GitHub’s privacy statement.
