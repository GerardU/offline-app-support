Pre-release / TestFlight. BagMosaic is being tested and is not publicly released. Features and availability may change before release.

A personal packing organizer

BagMosaic helps one person organize a household’s packing. It does not provide live collaboration, travel bookings, weather forecasts or destination-specific advice. Templates are editable suggestions; review your own list before departure. Keep a backup of data you need. This website does not sell an app or take payments.

Optional one-time purchase

The free app includes one active trip, the weekend template, basic packing, archived-trip viewing and backup. The optional BagMosaic Pro lifetime purchase unlocks additional active trips, the family holiday, camping and business templates, reusable personal templates and PDF sharing/printing. It has no recurring subscription charge. Apple displays the current price and currency before purchase.

Purchases and refunds follow Apple’s applicable processes. Restore purchases is available from Settings → BagMosaic Pro using the Apple Account that made the purchase. Internet is needed for purchase and restore. Saved packing data is not deleted when purchase access cannot be confirmed. See Apple’s refund instructions if you need to request a refund; eligibility is determined by Apple.
