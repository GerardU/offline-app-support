Using PanelMark

Enter rectangular wall dimensions and choose a batten count. Compare equal clear gaps, choose the left or right wall edge as zero, follow numbered marks, then check each cut and export the installation PDF. All vertical members fit between full-width top and bottom rails in this layout scheme.

One saved wall includes all calculations, marks, cuts and PDF exports free. Delete or edit it to plan another. Lifetime adds multiple walls, room/project grouping, duplication and reusable presets. No subscription.

Purchase and restore

The first purchase and restore need an internet connection. Use Settings → Restore purchases with the Apple account used for the purchase. Existing plans and exports remain available if ownership cannot be verified.

Local backups and recovery

Settings exports a JSON backup through the native share sheet. Import validates all walls before replacing the library and keeps a pre-import recovery copy. Restore the last recovery copy in Settings. Deleting walls or local data also keeps a recovery copy; uninstalling the app removes app-owned local files. Back up through Files before uninstalling. Backup limits: 200 walls, 100 presets, 16 MiB; maximum 100 interior battens per wall.
