Wall plans stay on your device

PanelMark stores wall dimensions, names, projects, presets, units, reference edges, completion checks and preferences in an app-owned local database. PDFs and recovery backups are app-owned files. Wall records are not sent to a product server. The app has no login, advertising, tracking, analytics SDK or remote catalog.

Optional lifetime commerce

Apple processes payment. RevenueCat validates purchase receipts and recognizes lifetime ownership, using a generated anonymous app identifier, purchase/transaction history and technical context such as app/SDK version, operating system, locale and currency. These requests need connectivity for purchase/restore. Purchase history supports app functionality and RevenueCat’s purchase dashboard. We do not attach a name, email address or advertising identifier, use customer attributes, or enable advertising integrations. Your wall content is not included in commerce requests.

See RevenueCat privacy information and Apple privacy information for their processing and retention.

Exports, deletion and backups

You choose where Files/share exports go and which app receives them. The destination handles the exported copy under its own policies. Import never restores purchase ownership. Delete local data removes the database records and preferences but keeps a recoverable local copy. Uninstalling removes app-owned data and recovery files. Device backups controlled by iOS or your backup settings may include local app data; externally exported backups remain where you saved them.

Support and this website

If you email support, your message and address are used to answer the request. Please do not include passwords, payment credentials or private wall records unless necessary. This static site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process ordinary web request information; see GitHub’s privacy statement.
