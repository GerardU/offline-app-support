Decorative layout planning

PanelMark covers rectangular walls with vertical decorative battens and optional perimeter rails/stiles. It provides no structural or building-code advice, AR measurement, general CAD, material catalog, pricing or stock-length optimization. Confirm dimensions, reference edge, display rounding and your actual joinery before marking or cutting.

Free and lifetime access

One saved wall with its calculations, marks, cut checklist and usable PDF is free. Optional lifetime access is a non-consumable one-time App Store purchase, not a subscription. Store-returned localized price is shown before purchase. Apple handles payment and refund requests. Restore requires the purchase account and internet. Your existing wall data and exports remain available when ownership is unknown or revoked.
