Pre-release information: Roundfolio is being tested. It has not been released on the App Store. Final release testing and privacy disclosures are still being completed.

Keep a score sheet

Start a match with 2–8 players or 2–4 teams. Choose highest total, lowest total or a positive target. Enter a signed score for every competitor before saving a round. Zero is a score; an empty field is incomplete. You can edit an earlier round and undo the most recent scoring edit.

Back up and restore

Open Settings, then Backup. Export all records and use the iPhone share sheet to save the JSON file to Files or another destination you choose. Export includes older completed matches even when only the latest three appear in free history.

To restore, choose a backup file, review the preview and confirm replacement. Import replaces the current records; the app keeps a local recovery copy of the pre-import data. Keep a separate exported backup before changing or deleting data.

Optional Pro purchase

Pro is designed as one lifetime purchase for full history browsing, reusable scoring templates and completed-match rivalry statistics. The in-app purchase screen shows Apple's localized price. Restore Purchases uses the Apple account that bought the unlock and requires a connection. TestFlight purchase and restore verification is still in progress.

Local data and deletion

Settings → Delete all local data removes this installation's score records, templates, preferences and local recovery copies after confirmation. It does not remove exported files, device backups or Apple/RevenueCat purchase records. Manage exported copies separately in their chosen destination.
