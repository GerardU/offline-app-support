Roundfolio: Score Keeper — Privacy information

SupportPrivacyTermsAll apps

Pre-release information: Roundfolio is being tested. It has not been released on the App Store. Final release testing and privacy disclosures are still being completed.

Score data stays in the app

Roundfolio stores player or team names, matches, scores, templates, preferences and recovery copies in a local SQLite database. The app has no user account, app backend, advertising SDK or app-level cloud sync. Score-sheet content is not sent to RevenueCat by the app. Sharing or exporting a backup sends it only to the destination you choose. Device backups provided by Apple are separate from app-level sync.

Commerce uses Apple and RevenueCat

The app connects to RevenueCat to retrieve purchase information, display products and check access. It uses a RevenueCat-generated anonymous customer identifier rather than an app login. The installed SDK sends app, operating-system, device and locale metadata, including Apple's identifier for vendor. This commerce communication can occur when the app opens, before any purchase.

The current RevenueCat dashboard retains anonymous customer records with first/last-seen information, app and SDK versions, locale and a country label. The app does not set contact attributes or advertising identifiers, and no optional RevenueCat integrations were active in the project audit on 29 September 2026. An SDK device identifier is not the same as advertising tracking.

Apple handles payment information. RevenueCat processes purchase history to validate transactions, provide purchase access and support its commerce dashboard. See RevenueCat's privacy information and Apple's privacy information.

Retention and current audit limits

Local records remain until you remove them. Settings → Delete all local data also clears local recovery copies; exported files and device backups must be managed separately. Deleting local scores does not delete purchase records. Contact support for questions about commerce records.

The final privacy declaration remains under review, including how the SDK's device identifier is retained and classified. This pre-release page reports observed behavior and does not claim that no data is collected or that the complete privacy audit has passed.
