Pre-release information: Roundfolio is being tested. It has not been released on the App Store. Final release testing and privacy disclosures are still being completed.

Roundfolio is a general-purpose score sheet for games played outside the app. It does not supply licensed game rules, online multiplayer or wagering. You are responsible for checking entered scores and retaining backups of records you need.

The free app is designed to include unlimited basic matches, teams, resume, the latest three completed matches in history, and backup/export of all records. Optional Pro is a one-time non-consumable purchase for full history browsing, reusable templates and completed-match rivalries. It is not a subscription. Product price is shown by Apple on the purchase screen.

Apple's applicable purchase and refund processes apply to App Store transactions. This pre-release website does not offer an app purchase, promise public availability or replace Apple's applicable terms. Final app terms and release verification must be completed before public release.
