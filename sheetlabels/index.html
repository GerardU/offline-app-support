SheetLabels support

SheetLabels turns CSV product rows into label-sheet PDFs. This app is in development; it has not been submitted or released.

Print at actual size

Choose A4, Letter or custom geometry, validate bounds, and print the 100 mm calibration PDF first. Set Actual Size / 100%, with Fit to Page disabled. Measure the reference before printing labels. User layouts are not vendor-certified.

Keep used positions accurate

The first physical sheet can have occupied positions. Subsequent sheets start blank unless another mask is supplied. Generating a PDF does not mark labels printed; confirm after printing, and use Undo if necessary.

CSV troubleshooting

Confirm comma, semicolon or tab. Correct every invalid row; none is silently discarded. Preserve quantities as positive integers. Prices are text under one selected currency, and SKUs retain leading zeroes.

Limits

Free exports: 24 labels. Lifetime Pro: up to 5,000 labels / 200 physical pages, saved mappings and reusable batch presets. Imports: 2 MB / 10,000 rows / 100 columns. Backups: 8 MB. Up to 50 batches/presets and 500 retained PDFs. QR content: up to 512 UTF-8 bytes, with modules at least 0.4 mm. Unsupported glyphs and text below the 8 pt minimum block export.
