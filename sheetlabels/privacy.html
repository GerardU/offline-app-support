SheetLabels privacy

This policy describes SheetLabels 1.0.0. The app is currently in development.

Product data

CSV content, mappings, sheet geometry, used-position masks, settings and generated PDFs stay in the app sandbox. SheetLabels does not include an account, catalog server, ads, app-usage analytics or runtime AI. QR content is generated locally, and URLs in imported data are not opened.

Purchases

The optional lifetime purchase uses Apple StoreKit and RevenueCat. Purchase and restore require connectivity. These services process purchase/entitlement records and an app-scoped anonymous identifier to recognize ownership. RevenueCat also provides the publisher with purchase-history reports (commerce analytics). SheetLabels does not set advertising identifiers, identifiable customer attributes or cross-app tracking integrations. Purchase services receive network information necessary to process requests. CSV content and PDFs are not sent to RevenueCat.

RevenueCat privacy details: RevenueCat privacy policy. Apple purchases: Apple privacy policy.

Files and backups

Import and sharing happen only through your actions. A backup contains CSV, mappings, geometry, masks and settings; PDF files should be saved separately. An atomic restore keeps a pre-import recovery copy. Undo recovery retains up to ten snapshots locally. Delete Local Data clears active product data with a recovery copy and retains exported PDF files. Device/system backups and destinations chosen in the share sheet are managed by you and the relevant providers.

Support

If you email mosartgames@gmail.com, the message and attachments are sent to that support mailbox. Do not attach private customer data unless you intend to share it.
