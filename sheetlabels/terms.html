SheetLabels usage terms

Draft for a pre-release app; not yet an App Store offering.

SheetLabels generates product-label PDFs from data you provide. It does not provide shipping postage, printer drivers, inventory synchronization or registered retail barcodes.

Check the original data, field mapping, quantities, page bounds and generated PDF before printing. Use the calibration reference and print at actual size. Physical printer accuracy and vendor-template certification are not claimed.

Free use includes up to 24 labels per export. Optional Lifetime Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, subject to the limits disclosed in the app. The store-returned localized price is shown before purchase. Apple handles payment and its purchase/refund terms apply. The app has not been released. Sandbox tests do not charge real money; public purchases become available only after Apple approval and the publisher’s release.

Keep copies of your data and exported PDFs. Contact mosartgames@gmail.com for support. First purchase and restore require a network connection.
